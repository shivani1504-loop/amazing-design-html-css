What's
Faëria?

Faëria is a blend of Collectible Card Game and Board Game. Build custom decks and shape the living battlefield with magical lands.

It's currently developed for PC, Mac and Linux.

How to

Deploy mountains, forests, deserts, and lakes to summon legendary creatures. Master positional strategy on an interactive hex grid battlefield.

Build the World as You Play

Unlike traditional card games, Faëria lets you shape the board. Place tiles to create ocean routes, mountain passes, or dark forests directly influencing minion paths.
